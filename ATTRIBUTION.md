# Third-party attribution

## Visuals, geometry and code

Everything rendered on the front page is original work for this site and is
generated in the browser: the thin-film transistor stack, the silicon lattice,
the electrolyte gel and its ions, the receptor sites, the wafer map, the
silicon-lattice tunnel and the CMOS layout are all procedural — geometry,
canvas-drawn textures, shaders and simulation. No third-party model, image or
video asset is used.

## Typefaces

License, served from Google Fonts.
