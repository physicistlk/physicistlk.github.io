## 3D model — "Space Station 4"

The fab tool shown on the front page of this site is derived from:

> **"Space Station 4"**
> by **re1monsen** — <https://sketchfab.com/re1monsen>
> Source: <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/space-station-4-cf80075368174bf9895f4fd266cf17e3>
> Licence: **CC BY 4.0** — <http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
> Requirements: author must be credited; commercial use is permitted.

The model is redistributed here under the terms of that licence, with the
credit reproduced on the front page itself (Scene IV — Epilogue).

**Modifications.** The model has been reinterpreted as a circular cleanroom
cluster tool: a central transfer module ringed by process chambers with
load-lock pods and service ports. The materials were re-authored (silver-grey
cleanroom hardware with lit indicator windows), the scene was lit from scratch,
a procedural wafer / diffraction backdrop and status-lamp ring were added, and
the camera choreography, the wireframe reveal, the process-flow decomposition,
the contrast-sampled dot-and-line field and the entire tunnel sequence were
created for this site. The original mesh structure and materials are preserved.

**Optimisation.** Textures were resized to 1.5K / 1K and re-encoded as WebP;
geometry was compressed with meshopt. No geometry was removed or added to the
source model.
