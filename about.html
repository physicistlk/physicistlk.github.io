Biography

Professional Profile

Bridging fundamental semiconductor device physics with scalable printed bioelectronics.

Lokesh Kumar is an experimental physicist dedicated to bridging the gap between fundamental semiconductor physics and scalable industrial applications. His work focuses on Organic Electrochemical Transistors (OECTs), oxide semiconductors (ZnO, IGZO), printed flexible neuromorphic devices, and bioelectronic sensors for environmental and health monitoring.

Currently serving as an Assistant Professor of Physics at NIT Calicut, he leads the Neuromorphic Bioelectronics Lab (NBL). His research career highlights include an IGSTC project collaboration with the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), advancing sustainable manufacturing processes in microelectronics.
