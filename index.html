What NBL does

Four research pillars

From charge transport in thin films to chips that learn and sense.

neurology 01 · NEUROMORPHIC

Synaptic transistors

STDP, paired-pulse facilitation and the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve in screen-printed OECTs at ~24 nJ.

 biotech 02 · BIOELECTRONICS

OECT biosensors

Protein-immobilised gates for phenolic water pollutants and DNA-binding proteins, at sub-nanomolar levels.

 recycling 03 · GREEN MATERIALS

Printed electronics & graphene

Ion-gel rGO transistors and microwave-assisted green graphene on flexible, printable platforms.

 sensors 04 · SENSING

Piezoresistive sensors

RGO-CNT-melamine foam transducers for pulse, strain and speech recognition with machine learning.
